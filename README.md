## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides insights into employee demographics, workforce distribution, employee experience, job satisfaction, and attrition patterns.

The dashboard helps HR teams understand workforce trends and identify areas that may require further investigation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and retention patterns.
- Monitor total and active employee counts.
- Understand workforce distribution across departments.
- Explore attrition by salary slab and job role.
- Analyze employee age groups and work experience.
- Compare job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Present key HR metrics in an interactive dashboard.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 👥 Total Employees
- ✅ Active Employees
- 📉 Attrition Count
- 📊 Attrition Rate
- 🎂 Average Employee Age
- 💼 Average Work Experience

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Department Analysis
Visualizes employee distribution and attrition across departments.

### 2. Salary Slab Analysis
Compares employee attrition across different salary ranges.

### 3. Job Role & Job Satisfaction
Displays job satisfaction ratings by job role.

### 4. Age Group Distribution
Shows the distribution of employees across different age groups.

### 5. Gender Distribution
Provides a visual breakdown of employees by gender.

### 6. Experience-Based Attrition
Explores the relationship between employee experience and attrition.

### 7. Department-Wise Employee Count
Compares workforce size across departments.

### 8. Interactive Department Filter
Allows users to explore the dashboard by selecting departments.
